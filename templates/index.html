How It Works

Every upload follows a verifiable flow from encryption through diagnosis and consented access.

1
Upload

Submit a medical image with your private encryption key.

2
Encrypt

The image is sealed locally with AES-256 before leaving your device.

3
Store

Encrypted payload is pushed to Robust Blob Store for resilient storage.

4
Record

Blockchain entries create a tamper-resistant audit trail.

5
Diagnose

AI models run securely when users provide authorized decryption keys.
